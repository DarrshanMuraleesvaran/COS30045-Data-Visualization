TELEVISION ENERGY

What influences how much energy a television uses?

TV energy consumption is shaped by more than one feature. Screen size, display technology and usage behaviour can all influence the final result.

THE BIG PICTURE

Not every television uses energy in the same way.

Two televisions can look similar but consume different amounts of electricity. Understanding the variables behind those differences helps make the data more meaningful.

↔

FACTOR 01

Screen Size

Larger screens generally require more energy to operate because more display area must be illuminated.

◫

FACTOR 02

Display Technology

LCD, LED-backlit LCD and OLED technologies differ in how images are produced and how energy is consumed.

☀

FACTOR 03

Brightness

Brighter screen settings can increase power demand, particularly during long periods of viewing.

◷

FACTOR 04

Viewing Time

A highly efficient television can still consume substantial energy when used for many hours every day.
