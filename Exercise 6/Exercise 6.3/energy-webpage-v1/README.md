# Exercise 6.3 Energy Webpage

This webpage extends Exercise 6.2 with a D3 scatterplot.

## Visualisations

- Histogram with filters.
- Scatterplot comparing star rating and labelled annual energy consumption.

## Scatterplot Encoding

- x-axis: star rating.
- y-axis: labelled annual energy consumption.
- colour: screen technology.

The scatterplot includes an SVG legend for LED, LCD, and OLED.
