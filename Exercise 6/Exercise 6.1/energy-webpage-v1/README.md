# Exercise 6.1 Energy Webpage

This webpage contains a D3 histogram for the Week 6 TV dataset.

## Visualisation

The histogram plots:

- x-axis: labelled annual energy consumption ranges.
- y-axis: frequency of TV models in each range.

- `index.html`
- `css/styles.css`
- `css/visualisation.css`
- `js/load-data.js`
- `js/shared-constants.js`
- `js/interactions.js`
- `js/histogram.js`
