# Exercise 6.4 Energy Webpage

This webpage extends Exercise 6.3 with scatterplot tooltips.

## Visualisations

- Histogram with filters.
- Scatterplot comparing star rating and labelled annual energy consumption.
- Tooltip interaction on scatterplot points.

## Tooltip

Hovering over a scatterplot point displays the TV screen size in a small SVG tooltip.
