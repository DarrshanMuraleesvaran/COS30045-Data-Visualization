SVG House & Garden

Exercise 4.1 explores SVG shapes, coordinates, fills, strokes, paths, groups and transformations through a house and garden illustration.

About the Illustration

This illustration is created entirely using SVG elements. Different SVG shapes are combined to build the house, garden and surrounding objects.

  • <rect>
  • <circle>
  • <ellipse>
  • <line>
  • <polygon>
  • <polyline>
  • <path>
  • <text>

SVG Coordinate System

SVG uses a coordinate system where the origin begins in the top-left corner of the SVG drawing area.

(0,0) is the top-left corner.
X increases towards the right.
Y increases downwards.
