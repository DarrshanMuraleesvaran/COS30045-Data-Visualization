WHY IT MATTERS

Energy use is often invisible.

Televisions are part of everyday life, but their energy use can vary considerably. Understanding those differences can help consumers make better-informed choices.

01

Display Technology

LCD, LED-based LCD and OLED televisions do not necessarily consume the same amount of energy.

02

Screen Size

Larger screens generally require more energy, although televisions of similar sizes can still vary considerably.

03

Energy Awareness

Looking beyond branding and specifications can help buyers understand the long-term energy implications of a TV.
