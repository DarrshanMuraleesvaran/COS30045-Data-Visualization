Does Display Technology Affect Energy Consumption?

TVs may look similar from the outside, but the display technology behind the screen can differ. This visualisation compares the average labelled annual energy consumption of LCD, LCD (LED) and OLED televisions in the dataset.

KEY TAKEAWAY

OLED TVs recorded the highest average annual energy consumption in this dataset.

LCD televisions recorded the lowest average annual energy consumption, while LCD (LED) televisions were higher. The result suggests that display technology may be associated with differences in television energy use.
